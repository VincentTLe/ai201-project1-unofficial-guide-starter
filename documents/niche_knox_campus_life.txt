## Niche Q&A (summaries generated from student reviews)
HOUSING: Students generally find the housing options varied but with some drawbacks. Freshman and sophomore dormitories tend to be older, sometimes lacking air conditioning and modern amenities, and some students report issues like fire alarms going off randomly or thin walls. Upperclassmen housing, such as apartments and suites, are described as nicer and include kitchens, which many students appreciate. The suite-style living fosters close-knit communities, but roommate assignments can be hit or miss, and some students wish for more kitchen facilities in dorms.

DINING: The dining options have improved over the years and are generally considered adequate, with a main cafeteria offering a variety of stations including vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, and allergen-friendly options. The campus also features a cafe and a grill/snack bar called the Gizmo, which serves fried foods and sandwiches, though some students find the food there less appealing. While the food is not considered gourmet, many students find enough variety to get by, with some specific favorites like pizza nights and themed meals. Some students desire healthier options and an upgrade in food quality.

PROFESSORS: Knox College is well-regarded for its dedicated and approachable professors who genuinely care about student success. The small class sizes facilitate personal attention, deep discussions, and strong student-faculty relationships. Many students report feeling both challenged and supported academically, with faculty willing to make time for students despite their busy research and teaching schedules.

SAFETY: Students generally feel safe on campus due to its small size and the availability of campus safety resources like a safety app and escort services for walking at night. Campus security works closely with local police. However, some concerns exist related to the surrounding town of Galesburg, including occasional incidents of harassment and safety issues particularly for female students crossing certain streets.

TOWN: The town of Galesburg is described as small and quiet with limited entertainment, dining, and shopping options, which some students find isolating without a car. The college tries to compensate with many on-campus events and activities, and students often use study abroad programs or excursions to nearby cities.

## Reviews About Campus Life (verbatim)

Rating 4/5 — Senior — Oct 6 2019
There is nothing particularly special about Knox on the surface. The campus is small and looks better in the summer than it does in the winter. Old Main (Knox's oldest building) is the backdrop to the famous Lincoln/Douglas debate. Attending Knox was the best decision I've made. In every class you take, your professor will know your name and care about your academic success. There are various clubs and activities. The people on this campus are friendly and proud. The library is gorgeous. The cafeteria food is average but the pizzeria nearby has amazing $5 pizza for students on Thursdays.

Rating 4/5 — College Sophomore — June 8 2015
Living on campus is fun because there's usually a lot going on. I lived in Conger/Neal and I'll be living in Post (which is so much better) in the upcoming year. The quads are fun because you have a ton of friends that live in your building, so there's always someone to hang out with.

Rating 3/5 — College Freshman — Feb 12 2015
Post Hall is the best dorm building to live in by far. The Quads are smaller and have shared bathrooms using the stall system rather than single bathrooms like in Post. The fire alarms go off randomly occasionally and there are no new amenities. The housing process went smoothly and the buildings are close to classes.

Rating 4/5 — College Sophomore — Aug 4 2014
I like the town of Galesburg; there are nice parks and enough attractions to keep one occupied. It's not a cultural mecca but considering it's a country town it does alright. The Carl Sandburg Home is here, and a railroad museum. On the far north of town there's a movie theatre; you'll need a friend with a car to take you unless you bike well. Lake Storey is pretty and has camping. Shopping is limited, there's a Target and a mall but it's not full of options. Some of my suite mates would take trips to Peoria to shop. Most of Galesburg is safe; there are some sketchy patches to the west of campus. But I feel safe here. The key to a good town/student relationship is getting out of the so-called "Knox bubble".

Rating 3/5 — College Sophomore — Aug 3 2014
The buildings except for the old dorm buildings are pretty ok. The classrooms are average - it would help to have bigger desks. The bathrooms are fairly roomy and clean. The basement of the cafeteria has a game room - some people play computer and video games there. The library is pretty big, has many computers, and can be a study area. GDH and Old Main have awful stairs.
