## Reviews About Students / Academics (verbatim)

Rating 4/5 — Junior — June 18 2023
I genuinely love Knox. The professors truly care about their students and despite them being busy with research/teaching, they will ABSOLUTELY make time for you. The school does a pretty good job hosting events and there are also many clubs that host events. Yes, Galesburg is small which is not for everyone but personally I enjoy it— food is pretty cheap/surprisingly good & the old architecture is beautiful. I have met amazing people at Knox, it's so easy to meet people (and get good housing!) since we are a small school. Finally, there are some issues with Greek life, but much less than at larger schools as only 30% of the campus is involved. Please do not write off knox just because it seems laid back or easy to get into. Most students here are extremely smart, hardworking, humble, and friendly, and the classes & extracurriculars are vigorous but rewarding!

Rating 4/5 — Sophomore — Dec 6 2022
The first year I was at knox I tried to transfer because I wasn't feeling challenged and I didn't think that there were enough opportunities at this small liberal arts college in Illinois. When I went back for my second year I realized two important things. The first was that if you have a pessimistic attitude you will get pessimistic results and the second was that at college you get out what you put in. During this second year I have made opportunities for myself by doing things like starting a club and joining a lot more clubs. Now there are genuine issues with Knox — it is in a small town that has some not great locals and the trains that pass through the area have woken up some of my friends, but for me I could get beyond a lot of that. If you're picking between a big school and Knox though I would pick Knox because the community you build is 100 times stronger.

Rating 1/5 — Freshman — Dec 6 2023
I would equate my time at Knox to slowly unveiling a lie and attempting to mentally justify it for comfort. Knox, to me, is a vastly polarized population of former tumblr users and student athletes signing their swan songs. This massive gap in ideology leads to a lot of distance between groups of people and drags the administration in a multitude of different directions. While some students would add their voices into the mix, the vast majority became tired of placing all focus on these issues when there was a very apparent lack of student resources on campus and the rising tuition prices to combat low enrollment. I have had good times at Knox, however the school holds onto a very entitled mindset while there are many miserable students who hear stories about Knox years ago being a thriving hub.

Rating 3/5 — Sophomore — June 2 2025
The financial aid committee for International Students is HORRIBLE. You tell them you have financial changes and cannot afford the 24k you are paying and they tell you to solve that on your own. The professors are good, great class sizes and coursework is well manageable.
