## Mark Shroyer — Physics
PHYS110, Jan 4 2019 — Quality 4.0, Difficulty 4.0 — Would Take Again: Yes, Grade: A
Exams with a massive amount of questions! The lectures are easy to understand and organized but he spends too much time on some very simple and basic questions, makes the class less effective. Grades fairly if you show diligence and passion through the term.

PHYS120, Jan 30 2018 — Quality 1.0, Difficulty 5.0 — Would Take Again: No
unclear lectures and exams are harder than others which is unfair. Tough Grader, Get ready to read, LOTS OF HOMEWORK.

PHYS130A, Jun 2 2017 — Quality 4.0, Difficulty 4.0 — Would Take Again: Yes
I really enjoyed this class. The homework sets are challenging but working through them really help solidify concepts. Tests and quizzes feature easier problems that involve the same concepts, so doing homework will help a lot. This class was definitely a challenge but Shroyer is great and willing to help students outside of class. Gives good feedback, Accessible outside class.

PHYS120, (online era) — Attendance Mandatory, Grade: Incomplete
literally expects you to teach yourself, and go to him if you have questions. in class he'll make you do problems from the textbook, while he slowly walks around the room "helping" everyone. his videos are slow and boring, and are barely enough to teach you about the subject. his class basically glorified office hours for an online class.

PHYS120, Feb 10 2023 — Quality 1.0, Difficulty 5.0 — Grade: D+
Extremely difficult to work with, not as understanding with circumstances. Tough grader, Participation matters.

## Peter Schwartzman — Environmental Studies
ENVS101, Jan 4 2017 — Quality 5.0, Difficulty 3.0 — Would Take Again: Yes, Grade: A-
GREAT TEACHER GREAT CLASS. Gives good feedback, Participation matters, BEWARE OF POP QUIZZES.

ENVS101, Apr 12 2019 — Quality 5.0, Difficulty 4.0 — Would Take Again: Yes
Peter is a wild and crazy guy, but so so so passionate. He kindles my love for the environment and makes me want to take action. Fun lecturer. Accessible outside class, Inspirational, Lecture heavy.

(ENVS, Nov 18 2024) — Grade: A
Nice and funny, good in discussion, but so much reading (60 pages a night) that was not related to the lecture. Co-taught with Sephora McIntyre but seemed like they really did not like each other and were teaching two unrelated courses. Lectures probably need updating since they've been in use for 15 years now.

ENVS228, Apr 22 2019 — Quality 3.0, Difficulty 3.0 — Would Take Again: No
Schwartzman is passionate, no doubt about that. However, he didn't use the texts we were required to read to guide his lectures or discussions. There was a huge disconnect between projects and his lectures' content. Somehow, every lecture ended up being about him and his experiences. It was supposed to be co-taught, would've been better that way.

## Joan Huguet — Music (100% would take again, 3 ratings)
(MUS, online/COVID era) — Would Take Again: Yes, Grade: A
I took several of Joan's courses while in college, including one remote (hey, COVID) course and an independent study, also online. She's an all-around amazing person, and that 100% carries over into her teaching style. She truly cares about every student that she teaches. Very accessible, her door is literally always open. Take a class with Joan! Accessible outside class, Participation matters.

MUS145, Feb 9 2019 — Quality 5.0, Difficulty 4.0 — Would Take Again: Yes
I've taken 4 courses with Joan. She's incredibly knowledgeable and always wants her students to succeed. Joan is always accommodating and works hard to help you in whatever way you need. Her classes are challenging. You will be pushed, but you will be proud at the end of the course. If you have time, take a course with Joan!!!
