### What Students Say (AI-generated summary by Niche)
Knox College offers a vibrant and diverse community that appeals to many students, particularly those seeking a close-knit, liberal arts environment. The small class sizes allow for meaningful interactions with professors, who are often described as passionate and supportive. Academic experiences are generally positive, though some departments, particularly in the arts, feel underfunded compared to others. Campus life is characterized by a variety of student activities and events, but the small-town setting of Galesburg can feel isolating for some, with limited off-campus entertainment options. While many students appreciate the strong sense of community and the availability of resources like career services and disability support, there are also critiques regarding the administration's handling of social issues and the overall campus bureaucracy. Tuition and financial aid are frequent topics, with some students feeling that financial support does not adequately meet their needs.

Rating 3/5 — Junior — 8 months ago
Knox is... okay. Their marketing and PR are incredible compared to what you actually get. I'm a transfer student here to study Art as I am getting significant financial aid, which was my deciding factor. In my opinion, their art department is hardly up to standard with other universities. There is basically one professor for all of Art History. Other friends of mine have been promised programs that don't exist, and/or their credits they came with were totally lost, prolonging their degree. Their programs are not equal, but they really push to make you think they are. There is an odd sort of cult mentality that surrounds Knox, and they're predatory towards low-income and international students. I love the social aspects, as my dorm and suitemates are great, and the food is generally good. All depends on what you study.

Rating 5/5 — Freshman — Mar 4 2025
Knox was my first choice school as I loved the diversity on campus. There are so many international students! Galesburg itself is a very small, quiet town with not a whole lot to do. The school makes up for this though by offering tons of fun activities and events on a weekly basis for students to attend. Because it is such a small school, everyone knows everyone. This is a good and bad thing, because if there is drama then everybody knows about it. The food is decent, with a pretty wide variety in what they offer on a daily basis. The campus coffee shop is also a nice perk, as well as the convenience store in one of the dormitories. At Knox you are required to live on campus all four years, and if you are in a dorm with no kitchen it can be inconvenient. That is the biggest change I would like to see at Knox, every dorm should be equipped with a kitchen for students to use.
